# Editorial measure review

All 257 Bulgarian editorial questions were submitted to the normal deterministic text provider against local data. The JSON companion records the requested wording, observed route/answer and disposition per question. A successful route alone was not accepted as proof of the requested measure.

46 questions are retained as reviewed searchable aliases of existing starters. Their routes and actual provenance were checked after repairs. Availability is per surface: a chat alias does not automatically become SQL-ready. The remaining questions retain explicit missing layers or ingestion contracts. These are limits of their exact wording, not blanket claims that a whole topic has no data.

## Implemented discoveries

- 55 macro indicators now have dedicated bilingual starters, categorized by their actual measure. This includes housing-price growth, homicide/prison rates, institutional trust, taxes, debt and labour measures.
- National water-services percentages now have a tool and starter. The answer separates annual national coverage from current settlement outages and network losses.
- Specific paraphrases route to budget variance, ministry budgets, filing compliance, hospital payments, medicine growth and other existing tools.
- Generic “who” no longer defaults to election results. An unspecified “my municipality” or “a specific institution” asks for clarification instead of fuzzy-matching words to a village.
- Election comparison fallback requires election context. Unsupported pension investment returns and individual heating-aid recipient counts no longer substitute asset totals or household counts.

## Exact-measure distinctions retained

Heating assistance counts households by season; the editorial question asking how many people is not promoted. COFOG social protection is broader than pensions. Pension-fund assets are not investment returns. National water rationing percentages do not identify settlements currently on rationing. Hotel nights by residence are not unique tourist arrivals. Selected coal plants are not a complete power-station register.

Existing university procurement needs institution selection and a scoped tool, not a new procurement ingestion. Housing-price growth and institutional-trust series already exist; a price-to-income calculation and a multi-institution trend question still need their own contracts. Homicide/prison series do not cover all registered crime. These narrower measures are now discoverable without claiming the broader editorial questions are answered.

The remaining editorial formulations are a tracked backlog. Their recorded diagnostic routes may be wrong or insufficient; the record deliberately does not mark them ready. This review does not claim that the heuristic router can correctly answer every formulation in the full editorial corpus, or that production data were refreshed.

## Review decisions

Both drafts now have question identity, round, wording, original population base, source locations, publication dates, translated methodology and resolved candidate identities. The residuals are visual transcriptions of printed values, not values calculated as 100 minus the other rows. Both distributions happen to total 100% including their published residual.

Scoring remains disabled under the current requirement for an explicitly decided-voter or valid-vote base. The 2021 treatment of the no-candidate response is additionally unknown. These are valid campaign observations; the later accuracy step must retain the documented denominator limits rather than manufacture comparable grades.

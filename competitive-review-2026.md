# Competitive Review — electionsbg.com vs. civic-data platforms (research snapshot: Aug 2026)

Scope: what each competitor actually offers now, their cross-dataset **linkage model**, and the move worth stealing. Every factual claim carries a source link.

## 1. Bulgaria

**SIGMA (sigma.midt.bg)** — the big new entrant. Launched **16 June 2026**: open-source register of **~193,000 contracts, >€51bn, 4,400 institutions, 17,000 companies**, six years of history, updated continuously, built in a month with AI assistance ([MREAST](https://www.mreast.eu/2026/06/bulgaria-launches-sigma-procurement-transparency-platform/), [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/economy/1148242-open-source-sigma-platform-brings-real-time-transparency-to-bulgaria-s-public-pr), [Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/229371/view_news.php?id=239053&disable_mobile=true)). **No risk detection shipped yet** — the roadmap is explicit: Phase 2 = commercial-registry + internal financial flows → ownership links; Phase 3 = preferential-treatment detection + automated alerts ("early warning mechanism", per PM Radev); a separate "AI tool for public procurement" was presented to the Council of Ministers ([BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/bulgaria/1148146-cabinet-unveils-sigma-ai-tool-for-public-procurement)). Context stat: >30% of contracts awarded with a single bidder ([BNT](https://bntnews.bg/news/minister-of-innovation-and-digital-transformation-more-than-30-of-public-procurement-contracts-were-awarded-with-the-par-1398771news.html)). **Overlap**: SIGMA covers the same procurement + company ground; its ownership-link and detection phases are roadmap rather than shipped, and the register is open-source.

**Диагноза България (diagnoza.bg)** — new 2025 platform by the "Black Track" author; claims **BGN 605M+ of suspicious healthcare spending** via anomaly signals ([BNT](https://bntnews.bg/news/author-of-black-spots-uncovers-more-than-bgn-605-million-in-suspicious-healthcare-spending-through-new-platform-1402100news.html), [BTA launch](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/1162481-bulgarian-developer-launches-healthcare-transparency-platform)). NHIF leadership met the creator; institutions requested API access to the signals ([BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/bulgaria/1164187-nhif-leadership-meets-creator-of-diagnosis-bulgaria-platform-to-discuss-healthca), [Fakti](https://fakti.bg/en/bulgaria/1067184-martin-atanasov-instituciite-poiskaha-dostap-do-signalite-ot-diagnoza-balgaria)). Direct comparator to the НЗОК hospital-payments sector pack — the differentiator is **signal curation + institutional uptake**, not raw data.

**Odis (INSAIT + Сметна палата)** — AI assistant for procurement auditors on EOP data; BgGPT extracts/structures/summarizes; cuts a 4-day audit to ~1 day ([Sofia University](https://uni-sofia.bg/index.php/eng/news/news_and_events/insait_and_the_national_audit_office_launch_an_ai_powered_public_procurement_audit_platform), [BNAO](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/en/news/press-releases/the-bnao-introduces-artificial-intelligence-in-public-procurement-audits/), [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/economy/1135224-bulgaria-s-national-audit-office-will-use-ai-assistant-in-public-procurement-ins)). Internal tool, not public — but legitimizes AI-on-procurement in Bulgaria.

**BIRD Public Money Scanner (scan.bird.bg)** — live investigative site by Prosto.bg/BIRD ([scan.bird.bg](https://scan.bird.bg)); known for PEP-property and company-hiding investigations ("[The Corruption Cyrillic](https://bird.bg/en/the-corruption-cyrillic-how-the-government-hides-companies-of-the-powerful/)", "[politicians' properties](https://bird.bg/en/imoti-politici/)"). Media-driven dossiers, no public resolved-ID layer.

**ИПИ: 265obshtini.bg + regionalprofiles.bg** — 265obshtini: per-municipality indicator series (EU-fund payouts 2007–2024, FDI, salaries, RES capacity to 2026) ([265obshtini.bg](https://265obshtini.bg)); regionalprofiles: **75 indicators** per district + annual reports + thematic analyses, including matura-exam (БЕЛ) results by district (June 2026) and road quality (July 2026) ([regionalprofiles.bg](https://www.regionalprofiles.bg/en/)). Pure indicator tables — **no entity/place linkage**, but the matura/outcome analysis overlaps the education sector pack.

**data.egov.bg** — official CKAN open-data portal; raw departmental registers, no entity resolution or cross-source joining ([Atlas](https://atlas.co/data-portals/data-egov-bg/), [UNECE case study](http://www.unece.org/fileadmin/DAM/env/pp/a_to_i/TFAI_Case_studies/BG_OpenDataPortal.pdf)).

**Other new 2024-26 entrants**: ACF's **eumoney.bg** (crowdsourced flagging of irregularities in EU-funded projects — [TheMayor](https://www.themayor.eu/en/a/view/bulgarian-civil-group-launched-platform-for-reporting-corruption-and-misuse-of-eu-funds-8898), [ACF](https://acf.bg/en/antikoruptsionniyat-fond-predstavya-in/)); **bgparliament.io** (CLS Sofia; parliament proceedings since 2009, searchable — [cls-sofia.org](https://cls-sofia.org/tpost/h2da6ui491-bgparliamentio)).

## 2. International — linkage models

- **YouControl (UA)** — full company dossiers, **person↔company affiliation graphs**, monitoring alerts, PEP screening; sold to prosecutors and businesses; YC World extends graph abroad ([YouControl](https://youcontrol.com.ua/en/about_us/), [prosecutor purchase](https://most.ks.ua/en/news/url/hersonska-oblprokuratura-vitratit-90-tis-grn-na-dostup-do-sistemi-youcontrol/)). Lesson: **graph + change-alerts as a commercial product**.
- **OpenTender.eu / GTI** — procurement data for **35 European countries** normalized into one schema ([opentender.eu](https://opentender.eu/start?lang=en), [HAL paper](https://hal.science/hal-03796734v4/document)); per-country integrity dashboards; **iMonitor 2.0** risk methodology updated 2026, deployed in BG via TI-Bulgaria ([iMonitor](https://imonitor.govtransparency.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/D2.2-Updated-Risk-Assessment-Methodology_final.pdf), [TI BG](https://transparency.bg/i-monitor-in-bulgaria/)). Cross-jurisdiction comparability is what makes their indicators reusable elsewhere.
- **Integrity Watch EU (TI)** — MEP declarations + lobby meetings; new lobbyists tool (2024) and 2025 update on Commission meeting transparency ([TI EU](https://transparency.eu/who-really-has-the-eus-ear-introducing-the-new-integrity-watch-eu-lobbyists-tool/), [2025 update](https://transparency.eu/new-data-more-corporate-access-integrity-watch-eu-update-shows-commission-leaves-civil-society-in-the-dark/)).
- **foaf.sk (SK)** — still live "social network of firms": company graphs, financial statements, tax arrears ([foaf.sk](https://www.foaf.sk)); **znasichdani.sk** — defunct (connection fails); the consumer-product lineage (contract→company→owners→politicians) lives on in the Fair-Play Alliance contracts database and TI Slovakia's Otvorené zmluvy ([Spectator](https://spectator.sme.sk/c/20039389/linking-public-contracts-to-business-owners.html), [data.europa.eu](https://data.europa.eu/sites/default/files/report/2012_slovakia_znasichdani.sk_making_procurement_data_matter_more.pdf)).
- **OpenCorporates / OpenSanctions / OpenOwnership** — OpenCorporates: largest open company graph, but free-API access is being commercialized ([OC blog](https://blog.opencorporates.com/category/use-case/data-access/)); OpenSanctions: deduplicated sanctions/PEP dataset with an entity-scoring API ([2025 review](https://www.opensanctions.org/articles/2025-review/)); OpenOwnership: **winding down its global BO register** in 2025 ([OO news](https://www.openownership.org/en/news/evolving-from-the-open-ownership-register-to-increase-our-impact/)). Lesson: open identity graphs need a funding model; dedup/scoring is the reusable asset.
- **OCP Red Flags** — methodology guide (2024) + **Cardinal**, an open-source red-flag library ([guide](https://www.open-contracting.org/resources/red-flags-in-public-procurement-a-guide-to-using-data-to-detect-and-mitigate-risks/), [Cardinal](https://www.open-contracting.org/es/2024/06/12/cardinal-an-open-source-library-to-calculate-public-procurement-red-flags/)).
- **OpenSpending / FDP / OCDS** — budget-to-transaction linkage remains an *unresolved standards problem* (OCDS budget-source issues [#483](https://github.com/open-contracting/standard/issues/483), [#1304](https://github.com/open-contracting/standard/issues/1304); [Fiscal Data Package](https://fiscaltransparency.net/open-fiscal-data-package/)). electionsbg's budget→contract join goes beyond what the standards currently specify.
- **ProPublica** — Nonprofit Explorer (IRS 990 search + email alerts), Dollars for Docs (pharma→doctor payments), Trump Inc (entity network of Trump businesses) ([NP Explorer](https://www.propublica.org/article/nonprofit-explorer-gets-email-alerts-other-improvements), [DfD](https://www.kcur.org/dollars-for-docs-a-kcur-propublica-investigation), [Trump Inc](https://www.propublica.org/series/trump-inc)). Lesson: person↔money as searchable apps **with alerts**.
- **OpenSecrets / FollowTheMoney (US)** — contribution→politician→industry aggregation, 2024-cycle data live ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/candidates-officeholders), [FtM](https://www.followthemoney.org/our-data/our-data-in-action)).
- **TheyWorkForYou (UK)** — 2025: new **Votes** feature, voting summaries, and MP "Signatures" ([May 2025](https://www.mysociety.org/2025/05/19/theyworkforyou-votes/), [Oct 2025](https://www.mysociety.org/2025/10/22/voting-summaries-update-october-2025/), [Signatures](https://www.mysociety.org/2025/10/21/signatures/)). Lesson: **explain-the-vote** summaries on raw roll-call data.
- **LobbyFacts.eu / InfluenceMap** — EU transparency-register spend/meetings ([LobbyFacts](https://lobbyfacts.eu/files/attachment/lobbyfacts_brochure.pdf), [EDJNet](https://www.europeandatajournalism.eu/cp_data_news/who-lobbies-the-eu-institutions-lobbyfacts/)); InfluenceMap scores companies on policy alignment ([LobbyMap methodology](https://lobbymap.org/briefing/LobbyMap-Methodology-24422)).
- **2024-26 AI "answer-the-data" products** — BudgIT **Bimi** (Nigeria, Jan 2025): AI chatbot over public-finance data ([BudgIT](https://budgit.org/bimi-unveiled-a-new-era-of-ai-driven-governance-and-open-data-in-nigeria/), [TheCable](https://www.thecable.ng/bimi-budgit-unveils-ai-chatbot-to-enhance-access-to-public-finance-data/)); **GRASP** municipal-budget chatbots (arXiv [2503.23299](http://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2503.23299)); **OGD4All** LLM-over-open-gov-data (arXiv [2602.00012](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2602.00012)); plus BG's Odis and SIGMA AI tool. Lesson: conversational access is the standard expectation now, and grounded, tool-based answers (electionsbg's ~155 tools) are what make it trustworthy; ungrounded chat over public-money data is a liability.

## 4. Positioning conclusion

**What is genuinely unique.** No researched platform holds elections + roll-call votes + public money + a **resolved 134k-person identity spanning 16 role sources** + place indicators + retail prices + outcome measures on one joinable spine. The splits in the field: SIGMA (state, money-only, identity planned but unshipped), YouControl (identity graph, no public money or votes), OpenTender (money, no persons), ИПИ (indicators, no entities), bgparliament.io/TheyWorkForYou (votes, no money), Диагноза (health signals, no person layer). electionsbg is the only one researched whose answer to "who is this person, what did they vote, and where did the money go around them" is one query. That cross-domain person key is the distinguishing asset; SIGMA Phase 2 and OpenSanctions/OpenOwnership each approach the same problem from a single side, which is useful precedent for how to build it well.

**Four highest-value moves to adopt:**

1. **Public, subscribable anomaly alerts (Диагноза + SIGMA Phase 3 + iMonitor lesson).** The 2025-26 wave is signals, not tables: Diagnoza's BGN 605M claims and institutional API demand, SIGMA's planned "early warning", iMonitor's red-flag training. electionsbg already computes risk masks (incl. НКИД→CPV mismatch, single-bidder, MP-tied firms) — ship them as per-company/per-person/per-sector "what changed this week" alerts, with an API others can subscribe to. This converts the data asset into the institutional-uptake asset.
2. **TheyWorkForYou-style explain-the-vote layer.** mySociety's 2025 Votes/summaries work shows the consumer product on raw roll-calls is explanation, not listing. electionsbg has 16,741 items / 4M casts; adding per-MP voting summaries and "what this motion did" (joined to the topic, the lobby contacts, and the companies it touches) closes the widest gap between what the roll-call corpus holds and what a reader can currently use.
3. **Open-source the risk methodology as a library (OCP Cardinal model).** Publish electionsbg's corruption-risk index (flags, masks, thresholds, reconciliation rules) as a documented, versioned, licensed open library. A flag that fires on a named company is a public claim, and today it is unfalsifiable from outside — the definitions exist only in code. Cardinal shows the shape: a spec plus a reference implementation, mapped onto the OCP flag vocabulary so a local calibration is legible to anyone already working in those terms. Plan: `docs/plans/procurement-risk-open-source-v1.md`.
4. **Grounded conversational "ask the budget/municipality" (BudgIT Bimi / GRASP lesson).** Chat over public money is now table stakes in civic tech, and generic chat is a liability — grounded, tool-backed answers (the ~155 tools) are the advantage. Point it at the budget→contract→company→person spine and at municipalities, and publish it as the reference "ask the data" UX for Bulgaria.

PartnerSearch Forum under the Call for direct support to SMEs

07.04.2025

On April 15, 2025, Tuesday, at 13:00 EET, in the Municipal Council Hall of the Municipality of Vratsa, a Partner Search Forum will be held under the call for direct support to micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.

The event, organized within the framework of the information campaign, provides an opportunity for organizations that wish to apply, but do not yet have established partnership relationships, to establish contact with other interested organizations.

Deadline for registration: April 14, 2025, Monday, by 17:00 EET. 

Participation in the Partner Search Forum provides additional advantages to participants: 

• Identification of potential partners: The Forum provides the opportunity to meet with other organizations or experts interested in similar areas of activity. This allows you to find potential partners for future projects and create cooperation that may be useful for your proposal.

• Exchange of ideas and experience: The Forum provides a platform for exchanging ideas and experience with other participants. You can learn about innovative approaches, successful practices and challenges that others have faced in similar projects. This can inspire you and help you build a better and more feasible proposal.

• Expand your network of contacts: Participating in the forum allows you to expand your network of contacts in your field of activity. You can meet project managers, experts, institutional representatives and other stakeholders who can be useful in future projects or provide you with support and advice.

• Access to information: The forum provides useful information on the application process, evaluation criteria and other details related to the call. This gives you greater clarity about the requirements and helps you prepare a better quality proposal. Pre-registration is mandatory (no participation fee). To ensure your participation, please fill in the relevant registration form.

The data you should fill in the form are as follows: 

• Participant name 

• Name of the organization you represent 

• Address of the organization you represent 

• Contact phone number 

• Email address 

Participating in the forum will help you increase your chances of a successful application and build valuable partnerships for future projects.

We look forward to seeing you!

Interreg projects for better disaster preparedness presented at the Annual Meeting of Bulgarian municipalities

16.10.2026

Floods, forest fires and extreme weather events do not respect administrative or national borders. Preparing for them, therefore, cannot be the responsibility of a single municipality or institution. It requires joint plans, well-trained teams and people who know how to act when danger arises.

05.10.2026

The 2014–2020 programming period of the Interreg-IPA Bulgaria–Serbia Programme has officially come to an end. The European Commission accepted its final implementation report, marking the completion of a period in which organisations from Bulgaria and Serbia worked together for the development of the cross-border region.

## Validation and limitations

- All 240 primary and all 50 repeat records pass the production validator and taxonomy/index checks.
- Article path, URL, domain, frozen membership, stratum ordering, repeat membership, and repeat allocation were checked exactly.
- Mentions were resolved deterministically with the production gazetteer in both passes. Their perfect repeat agreement measures pipeline determinism, not independent human entity-linking accuracy.
- This is one adjudicator's self-agreement pass, not an inter-annotator agreement study. It measures repeatability and surfaces taxonomy boundaries, but does not estimate between-annotator variance.
- Repeat summaries are deliberately excluded from scoring; the repeat files contain audit placeholders so reliability scoring focuses on the structured production fields.
- No files under `news/data/analysis` were created or modified.

Machine-readable completion metadata is in `news/data/gold/manifest.json`; repeat scores are in `news/data/gold/repeat/score.json`.
